# Client Deliverables vs. Internal Tools — The Distinction

## The Rule

The audit tool outputs are internal tools only. Clients never see them.

## Why

The robots-auditor, schema-validator, dom-audit, and sov-tracker outputs show every technical detail of what is wrong and how to fix it. If a client sees that report they can take it to any developer and fix it themselves for a few hundred dollars instead of paying us. We would be handing them a roadmap to cut us out.

What clients see instead is a translated deliverable — a clean professional report that shows them their scores, what the scores mean for their business, and what we are going to do about it. Not the raw tool output. Not the technical details. The business impact.

## What the Client Sees — Three Deliverables

### Month 1 — Technical Ingestion Report

Your website is now accessible to all 6 AI engines. Your AI Share of Voice baseline is 18%. Here is which engines are ignoring you and why. Here is our 90-day plan to fix it.

### Month 2 — Authority Footprint Report

Your brand now appears on G2, Crunchbase, and Trustpilot. Three independent publications mention your brand. Your Wikidata entity node is live. Your AI Share of Voice moved from 18% to 34%.

### Month 3 — 90-Day AI Share of Voice Report

Before: 18%. After: 71%. Here is every engine, every prompt, every score. Here is what changed and why. Here is what Month 4 through 6 looks like.

## What Stays Inside AEO Clarify

- robots-auditor.js raw output
- schema-validator.js raw output
- dom-audit.js raw output
- sov-tracker.js raw scoresheet
- All technical fix instructions
- All developer-facing remediation steps

## The Translation Layer

Every piece of raw tool output gets translated into one of three things before it reaches a client:

1. A score (number or grade — not the diagnostic detail behind it)
2. A business impact statement (what this means for their revenue, not their code)
3. A next step (what we are doing about it, not what they could do themselves)

The raw outputs stay inside AEO Clarify. The business impact story goes to the client. That is permanent.
