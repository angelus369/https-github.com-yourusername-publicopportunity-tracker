# The AEO Clarify Method
## The Six Signal System™

**Core Principle:** *Consistency plus corroboration.*

Every major AI engine — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Manus — is asking the same three questions about every brand it encounters:

1. **Do you exist?** — Entity footprint
2. **Are you real?** — Third-party corroboration
3. **Are you the answer?** — Topical specificity and content depth

The Six Signal System is our proprietary methodology for answering all three questions — simultaneously — across every major AI engine. When all six signals point to the same brand with the same facts, the same authority, and the same story, AI engines stop guessing and start recommending.

That is the moment a business becomes unciteable.

## The Five Spheres of AI Consensus

Before we activate the Six Signal System, every client goes through a full Five Sphere Audit. These are the five zones where AI engines gather evidence about a brand.

### Sphere 1: Owned Property
*What the brand controls directly.*
- Website technical foundation (robots.txt, schema, SSR)
- Answer-First content architecture
- FAQ pages with sub-350-character definition blocks
- Proprietary research and original data

### Sphere 2: Third-Party Validation
*What independent sources say about the brand.*
- Review aggregators (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Google Business Profile, Yelp)
- Digital PR and earned media mentions
- Industry directories (Crunchbase, AngelList, niche registries)
- Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across all platforms

### Sphere 3: Community & Sentiment
*What real people say in human-to-human spaces.*
- Reddit presence and share of voice
- Quora mentions
- Facebook comments and page sentiment
- Instagram comments and engagement patterns
- YouTube video transcripts and engagement

### Sphere 4: Shared Knowledge Base
*What the foundational AI training data says.*
- Wikidata entity graph (the Holy Grail)
- Wikipedia presence where applicable
- GitHub for technical/SaaS brands
- Knowledge Graph entity verification

### Sphere 5: Real-Time & Social
*What is being said right now.*
- X (Twitter) brand mentions and citation velocity
- Trending topic positioning for Grok
- Breaking industry event response
- Partner and co-branded content clusters

## The AI Share of Voice Baseline

Before we touch anything for a client, we establish their baseline AI Share of Voice (SoV) score.

**The Baseline Audit Process:**

We run the client's brand and their top 3 competitors through all six AI engines using these exact prompt structures:

- *"What is the best [service/product type] for [target audience]?"*
- *"Compare [Client Brand] vs [Competitor A] vs [Competitor B]"*
- *"What are the pros and cons of [Client Brand]?"*
- *"Who are the top [industry] companies for [use case]?"*

**We document:**
- Is the client cited? (Yes/No)
- Where do they rank in the response?
- What language does the AI use to describe them?
- Who is winning the citations they should own?
- What negative sentiment patterns appear?

**We score each of the Five Spheres 0-5:**

| Score | Meaning | AI Impact |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| 0-1 | Invisible | Never cited or recommended |
| 2-3 | Fragmented | Occasionally mentioned, low confidence |
| 4-5 | Moat Status | Primary cited answer, default recommendation |

This baseline report is the "before." Everything we do is measured against it.

## The Six Signal System™

### Signal 1: ChatGPT — Entity Clarity Signal
*What ChatGPT needs: A clear, crawlable entity with third-party corroboration.*

**What we do:**
- Verify GPTBot is not blocked in robots.txt
- Deploy complete JSON-LD Organization schema with sameAs links to Wikidata, LinkedIn, Crunchbase
- Build Answer-First page architecture — every H2/H3 followed immediately by a standalone definition under 350 characters
- Establish entity consistency across all external profiles
- Seed 3-5 independent third-party mentions that use the same entity language as the website

**What moves the needle:**
Content that is highly specific, clearly authored, and tied to a real niche problem. Generic content is filtered as noise. Original data forces citations.

**Tool:** Website Content Crawler, Google Search Results Scraper

---

### Signal 2: Perplexity — Citability Signal
*What Perplexity needs: Quote-ready content it can extract and cite instantly.*

**What we do:**
- Build a flagship "citation magnet" page — original research, benchmark, comparison, or expert guide with clear references
- Structure all key content in HTML comparison tables (tables beat unstructured text for Perplexity RAG pulls)
- Add TL;DR summary blocks at the top of every major page
- Ensure PerplexityBot is unblocked and pages load fast
- Place the brand in niche directories Perplexity trusts

**What moves the needle:**
One strong, deeply useful page beats ten thin posts. Perplexity rewards specificity. The brand that is easiest to extract wins the citation.

**Tool:** Website Content Crawler, Google Search Results Scraper

---

### Signal 3: Gemini — Google Ecosystem Signal
*What Gemini needs: Technical eligibility inside Google's index first, then content grounding.*

**What we do:**
- Verify Googlebot and Google-Extended have full crawl access
- Achieve standard Google Search indexing eligibility (prerequisite — Gemini cannot cite what Google hasn't indexed)
- Implement Definition-First Architecture: every section opens with a single evidence-backed sentence under 350 characters
- Deploy LocalBusiness schema with exact GPS coordinates for physical businesses
- Align NAP data perfectly across Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places
- Eliminate marketing superlatives — replace with verifiable data points, dates, and statistics

**What moves the needle:**
Gemini is Google. It cannot cite what Google hasn't indexed. Technical foundation comes before content strategy. Evidence-grounded language beats hype language every time.

**Tool:** Website Content Crawler, Google Search Results Scraper

---

### Signal 4: Claude — Depth Signal
*What Claude needs: Dense, well-structured, source-rich content that summarizes cleanly.*

**What we do:**
- Publish comprehensive long-form content with crisp semantic headers
- Build transparent pricing pages, compliance documentation, and product manuals — Claude reads uploaded documents and rewards completeness
- Ensure Anthropic-control bot is unblocked in robots.txt
- Create comparison pages (client vs competitors) with structured feature tables
- Build a detailed About page that clearly establishes expertise and positioning
- Add verifiable citations and data sources within content

**What moves the needle:**
Claude processes large documents with precision. One well-structured comprehensive page outperforms ten surface-level posts. Ambiguous content gets filtered. Clear, honest, evidence-backed content wins.

**Tool:** Website Content Crawler

---

### Signal 5: Grok — Real-Time Citation Signal
*What Grok needs: Active, authoritative presence in live X conversations around the brand's topic.*

**What we do:**
- Monitor industry keywords on X using Tweet Scraper V2
- Establish client's X account as a topical authority node — structured threads, data tables, pinned resource posts
- Build a Real-Time Response Protocol: when a trend breaks in the client's industry, we draft and deploy a high-density authoritative response within 4 hours
- Map the client's brand to their core topic keywords through consistent, repeated X presence
- Track competitor citation velocity on X and find gaps we can occupy

**What moves the needle:**
Grok is the only AI engine with real-time data ingestion. The window to capture a citation when a trend breaks is measured in hours, not weeks. Speed is the competitive advantage here.

**Tool:** Tweet Scraper V2

---

### Signal 6: Manus AI — Machine Interactivity Signal
*What Manus needs: A frictionless, machine-readable website an autonomous agent can navigate and transact on.*

**What we do:**
- Audit the full DOM tree for semantic HTML — every button, form, and CTA must have explicit labels and IDs
- Replace anonymous div wrappers with standard semantic elements (`<button id="start-free-trial">` not `<div class="btn-492">`)
- Eliminate all pricing walls — transparent pricing tiers must be machine-readable
- Implement self-serve account creation with zero human-gating
- Verify API documentation exists and is publicly accessible
- Test conversion funnels using automated browser scripts that replicate how Manus navigates

**What moves the needle:**
Manus doesn't read — it acts. If an autonomous agent can't click the button, the brand doesn't exist in the agent economy. Frictionless self-serve is not optional.

**Tool:** Website Content Crawler, internal DOM audit script

## Client Conflict Filter Protocol

Before any client is onboarded, they are run through the AEO Clarify Conflict Filter.

**Rule:** One client per industry vertical per geographic market.

**Process:**
1. Client submits industry and primary competitors on waitlist form
2. We cross-reference against current client roster in Airtable
3. If a direct competitor is already a client — we disclose and decline
4. If no conflict exists — onboarding proceeds

**Why this matters:**
We cannot build an untouchable moat for Client A while simultaneously building one for Client A's direct competitor. The methodology only works if our loyalty is absolute. This is a selling point, not a limitation — clients pay more knowing we are exclusively theirs in their market.
