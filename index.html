FAQ

Common Questions

Is it safe to give someone access to my email?

Yes. Every VA signs a strict NDA before they touch your inbox. We use read/write access with audit logging, not your login credentials. Our security practices are SOC 2-aligned, and you can revoke access instantly at any time.

Will replies actually sound like me?

Yes — that's the core of what we do. During onboarding, your VA studies your writing style, tone, and past emails. AI drafts + human editing means outgoing emails consistently sound like you. Most clients say nobody notices the difference.

Why Filipino VAs specifically?

The Philippines has one of the world's largest, most educated pools of English-speaking virtual assistants. Filipino VAs are known for strong communication skills, professionalism, and reliability. We go further — all our VAs are trained specifically in email management, inbox tools, and professional business communication.

What if my VA is sick or unavailable?

Every VA has a trained backup who's briefed on your account. You'll never experience a gap in coverage — a backup VA takes over seamlessly, with full context on your inbox and preferences.

Can I still access my own inbox?

Absolutely. You have full access to your inbox at all times — we're managing it alongside you, not locking you out. Most clients check in for 10–15 minutes a day to review flagged items and approve any sensitive replies.

How quickly can I get started?
