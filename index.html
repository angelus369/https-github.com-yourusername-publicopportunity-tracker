FAQ

Common Questions

Is it safe to give someone access to my real estate inbox?

Yes. Every VA signs a strict NDA before touching your inbox. We use secure delegated access — not your login credentials — with full audit logging. You can revoke access instantly at any time. Client and deal information stays completely confidential.

Will my clients and lenders know a VA is responding?

No — and that's by design. Your VA studies your writing style and past emails during onboarding. Responses go out in your voice, with your sign-off. Most beta agents say their contacts have actually complimented them on being more responsive.

Does my VA understand real estate deals and timelines?

Yes. Our VAs are trained on real estate transaction workflows — they understand the difference between a pre-approval email, an attorney review notice, and a closing doc request. They know what's urgent and what can wait.

What if a lead email needs an immediate response?

New lead inquiries are flagged as top priority. Your VA responds within the hour with a professional message keeping the conversation warm — and pings you immediately via Slack or WhatsApp so you can follow up personally if needed.

What if my VA is sick or unavailable?

Every VA has a trained backup fully briefed on your account. Coverage never drops — a backup VA takes over seamlessly with full context on your inbox, active deals, and preferences.

Can I still access my own inbox whenever I want?

Absolutely. You have full access at all times — we manage it alongside you, not instead of you. Most agents check in for 10–15 minutes in the morning to review flagged items. That's it.

Does it work with my CRM?

Yes — with the CRM Logging add-on, your VA logs key email interactions directly into Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, HubSpot, or whatever platform you use. Your pipeline stays current without you doing data entry.
