FAQ

Common Questions

What exactly is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO is the practice of optimizing your content and website so that AI answer engines — like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini — select and cite your business when answering user questions. It's the evolution of SEO for the AI-first search era.

How is this different from regular SEO tools like Ahrefs or Semrush?

Traditional SEO tools are built to help you rank in Google's blue-link results. They don't track AI citations, don't optimize for LLM retrieval patterns, and have no visibility into what AI engines say about your brand. Surfaced is built from the ground up specifically for how AI models select and cite sources — a fundamentally different technical problem.

Which AI engines does Surfaced cover?

We currently monitor and optimize for ChatGPT (OpenAI), Perplexity AI, Google AI Overviews / SGE, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot (Bing AI), and Claude. We'll be adding more as the landscape evolves.

Do I need to already have good SEO to benefit from Surfaced?

No. AEO and SEO are related but separate disciplines. Some sites with mediocre Google rankings get cited frequently by AI — and vice versa. Surfaced addresses the specific factors that drive AI citations, which includes but goes well beyond traditional SEO.

How quickly can I expect to see results?

Most beta users see measurable improvement in AI citation frequency within 2–4 weeks of implementing Surfaced's recommendations. Full optimization typically takes 6–8 weeks as AI engines re-crawl and update their knowledge.
