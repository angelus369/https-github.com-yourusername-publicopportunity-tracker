FAQ

Common Questions

Is NourishIQ a replacement for my doctor or dietitian?

No — NourishIQ is a meal planning tool, not medical advice. We help you eat in a way that supports your condition based on established nutritional science. Always work with your healthcare team. Many users share their NourishIQ plans with their doctors, who love seeing patients take initiative.

Do I need to know my lab numbers to use it?

Not at all. If you have recent lab results, entering them makes your plan more precise. But NourishIQ works just as well with just your diagnosis — we use evidence-based defaults for each condition.

What if I have multiple health conditions?

Our Plus and Family plans support multiple conditions simultaneously. Managing Type 2 diabetes and high blood pressure together, for example, requires specific food choices — NourishIQ handles the overlap so you don't have to.

Can I pick recipes based on foods I like?

Yes. During onboarding you tell us what you enjoy eating, what you can't stand, any allergies, and how much time you have to cook. NourishIQ only generates plans with foods you'll actually eat.

Does it work if I'm a picky eater or cook rarely?

Absolutely — we have "5-ingredient" and "15-minute meal" filters. If you eat out often, we'll also flag better fast food and restaurant choices for your condition.

What's the early access deal?

Everyone on the waitlist gets their first 3 months completely free on any paid plan, plus founding member pricing locked in forever. We're prioritizing people by condition group so you may be invited earlier if your condition is in our first batch.
