Yes. ContractCraft generates contracts using established legal language for each industry. They're enforceable in all 50 US states. How is this different from free templates I find online?

Free templates are generic and static. ContractCraft generates a contract based on your specific project — your client's name, your deliverables, your timeline, your revision limits, your payment schedule. It also includes niche-specific clauses that generic templates completely miss.

None. We ask you plain-English questions about your project and handle all the legal language behind the scenes. If you know what you're delivering and what you're charging, you can generate a contract.
