How is AuraDesk different from Upwork or OnlineJobs.ph?

On those platforms, you do all the work: post a job, screen hundreds of applicants, interview, manage, re-hire when someone quits. With AuraDesk, we handle hiring, vetting, training, and management. We also give your VA AI tools that multiply their output. And if your VA ever leaves, we replace them within 24 hours with no cost to you.

What makes an AuraDesk VA different from a regular VA?

Every AuraDesk VA is vetted from the top 5% of applicants and completes 40 hours of AI tools training before starting. They're trained in tools like ChatGPT, Notion AI, Zapier, and our proprietary AI workflow system — so they output 3× more than a typical VA who works manually.

What if I don't like my VA?

Simple: tell your account manager and we'll rematch you within 24 hours. No awkward conversations, no HR headache. We take the friction out of finding the right fit.

What hours does my VA work?

Your VA works your business hours — US Eastern, Pacific, Central, or international. We have VAs available across all major time zones. Night shift VAs are also available for 24/7 coverage plans.

Is my data and information secure?

Yes. All VAs sign an NDA before starting. We use role-based access controls so your VA only sees what they need. We never store your passwords — we use secure tools like 1Password Teams or LastPass for credential sharing.

Can my VA use tools I already have?

Absolutely. Our VAs are trained on 50+ common business tools: Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Asana, Trello, HubSpot, Salesforce, Shopify, QuickBooks, Canva, and many more. Tell us what you use and we'll confirm compatibility.

No. All plans are month-to-month. You can pause, scale, or cancel anytime with 7 days' notice. We earn your business every month by delivering results.
