Jurisdiction Variation

Contract law, freelance regulations, intellectual property rights, payment terms, and professional licensing requirements vary significantly across states and countries. A contract clause that is standard and enforceable in one jurisdiction may be unenforceable or even illegal in another. ContractCraft AI's templates are designed with general US legal principles in mind. If you operate outside the United States, or within a state with unique regulations affecting your industry, consult a local attorney before use.

When You Should Consult an Attorney

We recommend consulting a licensed attorney before using any contract for situations including (but not limited to):

  • Contracts valued at $10,000 or more
  • Long-term or ongoing service agreements
  • Contracts involving intellectual property transfer or licensing
  • Contracts in regulated industries (healthcare, financial services, legal, real estate)
  • International contracts
  • Employment or independent contractor classification questions
  • Any situation involving significant legal risk or dispute
