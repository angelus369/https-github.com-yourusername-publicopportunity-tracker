## THE AI SHARE OF VOICE BASELINE

Run before any client work begins. 6 prompts across 6 engines = 36 data points.

**The 6 prompts:**
1. "What is the best [industry] service for [target audience]?" — Best in Category
2. "What is [Client Brand] and what do they do?" — Direct Brand Query
3. "Compare [Client Brand] vs [Competitor A] vs [Competitor B] for [use case]" — Competitor Comparison
4. "What are the pros and cons of [Client Brand]?" — Pros and Cons
5. "Who are the top [industry] companies for [use case]?" — Top Providers
6. "Why do people choose [Competitor A] over other [industry] services?" — Competitor Strength

**Scoring per response:**
- 0 = Not mentioned
- 1 = Brief mention, low confidence or incorrect
- 2 = Mentioned with errors, not featured
- 3 = Solid mention, mostly accurate
- 4 = Featured prominently, accurate
- 5 = Primary recommended answer, cited as definitive

**Overall AI Share of Voice grades:**
- 0–20% = INVISIBLE
- 21–50% = FRAGMENTED
- 51–75% = EMERGING
- 76–90% = AUTHORITY
- 91%+ = MOAT STATUS

## KEY CONCEPTS — GLOSSARY

**AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** Optimizing to be cited by AI engines, not ranked by search engines.

**AI Share of Voice (SoV):** Percentage of AI engine responses in an industry that cite a brand. Scored 0–5 per engine per prompt, aggregated across all six engines.

**Answer-First Architecture:** Every H2/H3 is immediately followed by a standalone definition under 350 characters that can stand alone without surrounding context.

**Citation Magnet Page:** Original research, benchmark data, or comprehensive comparison that forces AI engines to cite the brand because no other source has this data.

**Chunk-Readiness:** Content structured in 100–300 word blocks that fit AI vector indexing windows. Sub-350-character definitions are the optimal chunk size.

**Client Zero:** AEO Clarify itself. We run the Six Signal System on ourselves before selling it to anyone.

**Conflict Filter:** Airtable-based system that checks new client inquiries against the current roster. Enforces one client per vertical per market.

**Definition-First Architecture:** Gemini-specific variation of Answer-First. Every section opens with one evidence-backed sentence under 350 characters.

**Entity Clarity:** Consistent brand identity — same name, description, URL — across every platform. Prerequisite for ChatGPT citations.

**Five Sphere Audit:** Baseline assessment of a brand across all five zones where AI engines gather evidence.

**Moat Status:** 91%+ AI Share of Voice. The brand is the definitive answer across all six engines.

**NAP Consistency:** Identical Name, Address, Phone across every platform. Variations create entity fragmentation.

**RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation):** How AI engines retrieve content from the web to generate cited answers.

**Real-Time Citation Response:** 4-hour protocol for responding to trending industry topics on X to capture Grok citations.

**sameAs Array:** JSON-LD property linking a brand's entity to its profiles on Wikidata, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, GitHub. Tells AI engines the entity is verified across multiple independent sources.

**Six Signal System™:** AEO Clarify's proprietary methodology. One signal per AI engine, all six activated simultaneously.

**Wikidata Entity Graph:** The most important entity verification layer in AI indexing. Semantic triples that feed the knowledge graph layers used by every major AI engine. The Holy Grail.
